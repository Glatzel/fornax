# LJPEG hypothesis measurements

Method:

- Release optimization, one Rayon worker, page-cache-hot inputs.
- Real files: three fresh processes per variant with five timed rounds each
  after one warmup. Fifteen observations per mean.
- Synthetic matrix: one process with seven rounds per case after a mandatory
  exact-sample verification pass.
- All variants retained the exact decoded sample bits.

## Real-file results

| B: const components | 267.899 ± 1.883 | 242.700 ± 2.853 | -9.40% | 559.753 ± 17.646 | 501.641 ± 8.951 | -10.38% |
| C: buffered errors | 267.899 ± 1.883 | 203.723 ± 4.222 | -23.95% | 559.753 ± 17.646 | 418.696 ± 6.636 | -25.20% |
| D: table reuse | 267.899 ± 1.883 | 271.881 ± 4.026 | +1.49% | 559.753 ± 17.646 | 558.268 ± 8.778 | -0.27% |

The final interleaved all-case run measured FlashRaw at 96.864 ms for 5D3,
194.305 ms for Pixel 8, and 41.023 ms for iPhone SE. Final Fornax/FlashRaw is
2.09x on 5D3, 2.18x on Pixel 8, and 1.84x on iPhone SE.

The iPhone SE final Fornax measurement is 75.435 ± 6.054 ms versus 107.3 ms in
the pre-optimization report, a 29.7% reduction. Pentax K-5 remains unmeasured
because its corpus file is a pointer in this checkout and Fornax has no Pentax
container decoder.

## Decisions

- Kept C: `Result` construction/propagation is removed from every hot sample.
  The lookup reports unusual codes through a buffered error flag and the scan
  checks it once after the row loop. Invalid-code behavior is covered by a
  direct regression test.
- Reverted A: isolated real-file gains do not compose with C. A+B+C regressed
  noisy synthetic scans by 12-16% versus baseline, and A+C regressed them by
  14-25%.
- Reverted B: it is independently fast, but B+C is 1.4% slower on 5D3 and 1.1%
  slower on Pixel 8 than C alone, at or within the larger run spread. C alone is
  simpler and has the strongest real-file result.
- Reverted D: table reuse regressed 5D3 by 1.49%, beyond the 0.70% baseline
  spread, without a distinguishable Pixel 8 gain.
