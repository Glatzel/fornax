# DNG remaining-gap perf counters

Host and method:

- WSL2 Debian, AMD Ryzen 7 5800H.
- Release optimization with DWARF debug info.
- `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, and FlashRaw `threads = Some(1)`.
- Page-cache-hot inputs.
- Each process ran one warmup decode and then the listed measured decodes.
- Counters are normalized per measured decode from the raw `perf stat` totals.

| Case | Decoder | Rounds | Instructions/decode | Branch misses/decode | Minor faults/decode |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 5D3 uncompressed | Fornax | 5 | 6,465,035 | 23,888 | 12,275 |
| 5D3 uncompressed | FlashRaw | 5 | 7,270,406 | 7,503 | 1,500 |
| 5D3 LJPEG | Fornax | 5 | 3,644,274,778 | 4,732,119 | 16,635 |
| 5D3 LJPEG | FlashRaw | 5 | 951,622,276 | 600,425 | 1,176 |
| Pixel 8 LJPEG | Fornax | 3 | 7,198,107,800 | 11,886,112 | 41,228 |
| Pixel 8 LJPEG | FlashRaw | 3 | 1,934,504,549 | 1,707,856 | 2,026 |
| 5D3 FP32 Deflate | Fornax | 5 | 3,029,607,506 | 11,674,591 | 23,787 |
| 5D3 FP32 Deflate | FlashRaw | 5 | 3,837,188,283 | 6,252,994 | 1,789 |

Interpretation:

- 5D3 LJPEG executes 3.83x FlashRaw's instructions and incurs 7.88x its
  branch misses.
- Pixel 8 LJPEG executes 3.72x FlashRaw's instructions and incurs 6.96x its
  branch misses.
- 5D3 uncompressed executes fewer instructions than FlashRaw but incurs 8.18x
  its minor faults.
- FP32 Deflate also executes fewer instructions than FlashRaw but incurs 13.30x
  its minor faults.

The raw `perf stat -x,` output is reproducible with the commands in
[the implementation plan](../../../plans/chore-close-dng-flashraw-gap.md).
